## Exercice {nump}`exercice`

Effectuez.

{.lower-alpha-paren}
1.  $(ax+3x-3) + (3x-4ax+2)=$
    {vspace}`2lh`
2.  $(ax+3x-3) - (3x-4ax+2)=$
    {vspace}`2lh`
3.  $(2x-5)\cdot (4x+2)=$
    {vspace}`2lh`
4.  $(x^2-y^3) \cdot (2y+4x)=$
    {vspace}`2lh`
5. $(a+b) \cdot (a^2+2) \cdot (3+b)=$
    {vspace}`2lh`

```{solution}
{.lower-alpha-paren}
1.  $(ax+3x-3) + (3x-4ax+2)=ax+3x-3 + 3x-4ax+2=-3ax+6x-1$
2.  $(ax+3x-3) - (3x-4ax+2)=ax+3x-3-3x+4ax-2=5ax - 5$
3.  $(2x-5)\cdot (4x+2)= 8x^2+4x-20x-10 = 8x^2-16x-10$
4.  $(x^2-y^3) \cdot (2y+4x)= 2x^2y + 4x^3-2y^4-4xy^3$
5.  $(a+b) \cdot (a^2+2) \cdot (3+b)= (a^3+a^2b+2a+2b) \cdot (3+b)$<br>
    $=3a^3+3a^2b+6a+6b+a^3b+a^2b^2+2ab+2b^2$
```


## Exercice {nump}`exercice`

Effectuez la division entière (divison en colonne avec reste).

{.lower-alpha-paren}
1.  $1612:13=$
    {vspace}`2lh`
2.  $1101:7=$
    {vspace}`2lh`
3.  $224:5=$
    {vspace}`2lh`

```{solution}
{.lower-alpha-paren}
1.  $1612:13= 124$ reste $0$
2.  $1101:7= 157$ reste $2$
3.  $224:5= 44$ reste $4$
```
